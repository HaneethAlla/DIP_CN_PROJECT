

## Objective

Inspect representative raw images from all 13 WBC classes before applying cleaning or preprocessing.

## Inspection Checklist

- Inspect representative raw images from every class.
    
- Look for differences in staining, brightness, contrast and background.
    
- Check whether the target WBC is clearly visible.
    
- Observe whether other blood cells or artifacts are present.
    
- Identify blur, noise, incomplete cells, overlapping cells and unusual crops.
    
- Record suspicious images before deciding whether any action is required.
    

## Status

Systematic visual inspection and its observations should be recorded after examining representative images from the local dataset.

Do not claim this inspection is complete until the images have been reviewed and the observations documented.
